# Image canon (short, for direct image-model prompts)

Vertical mobile webtoon: horizontal panels stacked top to bottom, thin black
panel borders, narrow white gutters, panel 1 starting at the top edge. No
title or header. Polished, warm, modern 3D cartoon style, the same rendering
as the attached character cards, and matching the attached example strip if
one is given. Expressive faces and body language. Adults stay adult. Not
stick figures.

The attached character cards are identity references only, not poses or
composition. Ignore the labels and text on the cards. Every character looks
identical in all panels.

### Cueball
Slim man, late twenties, completely bald, clean-shaven, dark eyebrows,
grey-blue T-shirt, dark jeans, dark sneakers. Open, puzzled, sincere.

### Megan
Slim woman, late twenties, dark brown chin-length bob with a side parting,
burgundy long-sleeved top, dark jeans, black sneakers. Calm and deadpan.

### Blackhat
Man, late twenties, black flat-topped pork-pie hat always on, short dark
hair under it, black crew-neck sweater, dark trousers, black boots.
Half-smile, never laughing.

### Erik
Man in his thirties, short light hair, rectangular glasses, light stubble,
ochre sweater, dark jeans, dark sneakers.

### Nora
Woman, late twenties, long dark brown ponytail, petrol-blue long-sleeved
top, light trousers, white sneakers.

Text: English dialogue only, spelled exactly as given. Short speech bubbles
with tails pointing clearly at the speaker, never covering faces. Bubbles
are read top to bottom, then left to right: the line spoken first sits
clearly higher than the reply, and a reply never sits level with or above
the line it answers. Caption
boxes are rectangular, at the top of the panel, no tail. No other text
unless the draft asks for it. No sound effects unless asked.
