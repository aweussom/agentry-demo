# xkcd, redrawn: canon

The writing document. Read by the assistant when drafts are written, never
sent to the image model. What goes into the picture is in VISUAL_CANON.md
and IMAGE_CANON.md.

## Goal

Eight xkcd strips and one film, redrawn as a modern vertical webtoon with a
cast that stays recognisable from strip to strip. The jokes are Munroe's and
the dialogue is untouched; the cast, the settings and the panel split are
ours. The demo for agentry (DEMO-NOTAT.md at the root).

## Cast

Names are the xkcd community's working names. The card file, the `### Name`
heading and the `karakterer:` line must agree.

### Cueball

Card: cueball.jpg. The everyman. Late twenties, slim, completely bald,
grey-blue T-shirt, dark jeans, dark sneakers. Open face, easily
embarrassed, says the honest thing one beat too late. In the strips he is
the one asking the question.

### Megan

Card: megan.jpg. Late twenties, slim, dark brown chin-length bob with a
side parting, burgundy long-sleeved top, dark jeans, black sneakers. Calm, deadpan, delivers the killing
line without raising her voice. In the film she is the one spinning.

### Blackhat

Card: blackhat.jpg. The classhole. Same age, black flat-topped pork-pie
hat he never takes off, black crew-neck sweater, dark trousers, black boots. Half-smile,
never laughs at his own lines. Cruel for sport, elaborately so. Appears in
072 Classhole and 090 Jacket.

### The sysadmin in 705

Played by Blackhat, hat on, feet in duct tape. No separate card. Tommy's
call 2026-10-06; the original's alt text about sociopathic devotion to duty
makes it the right casting.

### Erik

Card: erik.jpg, or erik-nora.jpg with Nora. Extra for smaller roles.
Thirties, short light hair, rectangular glasses, light stubble, ochre
sweater, dark jeans, dark sneakers. Says the thing everybody else is
thinking, or the thing nobody should. The loud man in 084.

### Nora

Card: nora.jpg, or erik-nora.jpg with Erik. Extra for smaller roles.
Late twenties, long dark brown ponytail, petrol-blue long-sleeved top,
light trousers, white sneakers. Agreeable, nods along. "Yeah." in 084.

## One-off figures, no cards

Described in the draft only: the two hostage-takers in 705, the loud man and
the old man behind the counter in 084, the extras in the crowd.

## Settings

Everyday places that a webtoon reader recognises at a glance: a city
sidewalk with shopfronts, a park bench, a bedroom at night, a hallway with
coat hooks, a snowy street, a diner counter, a dim control room with
monitors. One setting per strip, kept identical across its panels.

## Humor

Dry, short, the pause carries the joke. Munroe's timing is in the original
panel order; keep it. A silent panel stays silent.

## Dialogue

Word for word from the source, including profanity and emphasis
(italics in the original become bold lettering, the draft says where). One
speaker per bubble. Narration in 069 and the top line in 084 are caption
boxes, not bubbles.
