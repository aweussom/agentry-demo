Five panels, as in the original. Daytime city sidewalk outside a small
corner shop with a glass door; same spot in every panel. Megan has just
come back out of the shop carrying a small paper bag. Cueball has been
waiting on the pavement. Megan stands on the LEFT and Cueball on the RIGHT
in every panel, so the bubbles read in speaking order. The joke is the
straight face: nobody shouts, the pauses carry it.

Panel 1: Megan steps out of the shop door with the paper bag, stopping
short, eyebrows up. Cueball stands a few steps away, turning toward her
with a wounded, dramatic expression, one hand on his chest. Megan: "Why
didn't you wait for me?" Cueball: "I thought you were gone forever!"

Panel 2: Megan holds up one finger, flat stare. Cueball looks away, down
the street, a faraway look. Megan: "I said I'd be back in a minute!"
Cueball: "The... the seconds went fast at first, but then they started to
drag on." Cueball, a second, smaller bubble below: "She was there for me."

Panel 3: Megan leans in, mouth open, the bag forgotten at her side.
Cueball shrinks a little, eyes to the pavement. Megan: "You had an affair
in the 90 seconds I was gone?!" Cueball, small bubble: "...yes."

Panel 4: Same framing, both still. Cueball looks up at her, earnest.
Megan expressionless. Cueball: "And we had a son."

Panel 5: Same framing. Megan has not moved. Cueball, a little proud,
looking into the distance. Cueball: "He'd be about your age now."
