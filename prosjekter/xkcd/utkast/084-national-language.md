Four panels, as in the original. A diner counter. Behind the counter on the
right stands an old man in an apron: seventies, long grey hair tied back,
weathered face, calm; a one-off, no card. In front of the counter stand
four customers in a row, left to right: Erik (the one talking), Nora,
Cueball, and Megan nearest the counter. Same framing in every panel. Panel
1 has a rectangular caption box across its top with the hand-written line
"This happened to my friend:".

Panel 1: Caption box at the top: "This happened to my friend:". Erik
speaks with a raised hand, chin up. Nora nods. Cueball and Megan listen
politely. Erik: "English should be the national language. These
immigrants should have to learn English when they come here." Nora:
"Yeah."

Panel 2: Erik spreads his arms, lecturing. Megan glances sideways at the
old man behind the counter. Erik: "When you go to live somewhere, you
learn the language they speak there. English is the language of the
land."

Panel 3: The old man behind the counter leans forward on his hands and
speaks, mild face. All four customers turn their heads toward him. Old
man: "Excuse me, but osio Sarah dawado."

Panel 4: Erik frowns, confused. The old man is wiping the counter with a
cloth, not looking up. Megan hides a smile. Erik: "What the hell was
that?" Old man: "Cherokee."
