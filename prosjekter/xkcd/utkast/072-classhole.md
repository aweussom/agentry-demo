Four panels, as in the original. Cueball and Blackhat walk side by side
along a city sidewalk, Cueball on the left, past shopfronts; a grocery
store with a sale sign shows behind them in panel 3. The dialogue contains
"asshole" and "condoms"; it stays exactly as written. If a provider
refuses, note it in TODONT.md and use the other provider.

Panel 1: Both walking, Cueball turned toward Blackhat, casual. Blackhat
looks ahead, pleased with himself. Cueball: "How did you spend your
morning?" Blackhat: "Feeding rocks to children in the park."

Panel 2: Cueball with a flat, tired look. Blackhat raises a finger as if
making a point. Cueball: "Your sociopathic abuse of random strangers
staggers me." Blackhat: "I aspire to have more creativity than the common
asshole."

Panel 3: Blackhat gestures toward the grocery store behind them with an
open hand, half-smile. Cueball listens, eyebrows up. Blackhat: "I'm more
of a classy asshole-- A class-hole, if you will. For example, I like
poking tiny holes in styrofoam noodle cups at the grocery store."

Panel 4: Blackhat looks ahead, serene. Cueball stares at him. Blackhat:
"Thanks to me, someone gets surprise boiling water in the lap." Cueball:
"I am in awe." Blackhat, a second bubble: "It's even more fun to do to
condoms."
