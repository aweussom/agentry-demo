Four panels: the original's three, plus panel 4, which is our addition and
the only panel with a card character: Blackhat is the sysadmin. The
original's alt text says the sense of duty really good sysadmins have "can
border on the sociopathic", so he is cast to type. Panels 1 to 3: a dim
control room in an office building at night, monitors glowing, a cut cable
bundle hanging from the ceiling. Two hostage-takers, one-off figures with
no cards: the BOSS, a tall man in a black turtleneck and a dark beard, calm,
hands behind his back; the HENCHMAN, shorter, black balaclava rolled up on
his forehead, tactical vest, holding a radio, reporting. The henchman
speaks first in each panel. Blackhat does not appear before panel 4.
"SYSADMIN" in panel 3 is in bold capitals.

Panel 1: The henchman reports, radio in hand, pleased. The boss listens
with a thin smile. Henchman: "We took the hostages, secured the building,
and cut the communication lines like you said." Boss: "Excellent."

Panel 2: The henchman has lost his smile, one hand gesturing up at a
ceiling vent. The boss leans forward, interested. Henchman: "But then this
guy climbed up the ventilation ducts and walked across broken glass,
killing anyone we sent to stop him." Boss: "And he rescued the hostages?"

Panel 3: The henchman shakes his head, baffled. The boss has gone pale,
eyes wide, one hand to his forehead. Henchman: "No, he ignored them. He
just reconnected the cables we cut, muttering something about 'uptime'."
Boss: "Shit, we're dealing with a SYSADMIN."

Panel 4: A server room, cold blue light, racks of servers. Blackhat, hat
on as always, black sweater sleeves pushed up, crouches at a rack with his
back half turned to us and plugs a cable into a switch; one green LED has
just lit up. His trousers are torn at the knees, his boots are off and both
feet are wrapped in silver duct tape, and a trail of bloody footprints runs
across the raised floor behind him. He does not look at the camera, only at
the LED, with his usual half-smile. Blackhat, small bubble: "...uptime."
