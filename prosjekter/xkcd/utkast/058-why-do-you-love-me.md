Four panels, as in the original. A park bench at golden hour, a tree and a
path behind it; same framing in every panel, seen from the front. Cueball
and Megan sit side by side on the bench, Cueball on the left.

Panel 1: Cueball turns toward Megan, hopeful. Megan looks straight ahead,
calm. Cueball: "Why do you love me?" Megan: "I don't know; my heart never
gave me a choice."

Panel 2: Cueball melts, eyes soft, a hand on his heart. Megan unchanged.
Cueball: "Aww."

Panel 3: Silent panel. Both sit as before. Megan still looks ahead. No
text at all.

Panel 4: Megan, same posture, same calm face, eyes still ahead. Cueball's
smile has frozen. Megan: "I wish it had."
