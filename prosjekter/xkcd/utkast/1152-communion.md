Four panels, as in the original. A snowy residential street in early
January, grey sky, bare trees, a few houses with lit windows. Cueball and
Megan walk side by side toward the reader, Cueball on the left, both in
winter coats and scarves, breath visible. Same framing in every panel.
"Seriously" in panel 4 is in bold.

Panel 1: Cueball turns to Megan with a friendly smile. Megan looks ahead.
Cueball: "How was Christmas? Did you go to church?"

Panel 2: Megan answers matter-of-factly, eyes still ahead. Cueball
listens, nodding. Megan: "Yup. We celebrated the birth of a child, then we
ate of his flesh and blood."

Panel 3: Silent panel. Both keep walking. Cueball's smile has faded, his
eyes have slid toward her. No text at all.

Panel 4: Megan, same calm face, a small frown of genuine concern. Cueball
has stopped walking half a step behind her, mouth open. Megan: "Seriously
hope we got the right child this time."
