Three panels, as in the original. In the original Cueball tells the story
alone to the reader; here we show the bedroom and keep his words as
narration in rectangular caption boxes at the top of each panel. No speech
bubbles anywhere. A bedroom at night, dim blue light, a double bed seen
from the side.

Panel 1: Megan and Cueball lie in bed under the duvet, heads on the
pillows. Megan stares up at the ceiling. Cueball lies beside her, looking
at her from the corner of his eye. Caption box: "Staring at the ceiling,
she asked me what I was thinking about."

Panel 2: Closer on Cueball, propped on one elbow, looking straight at the
reader with a regretful, sheepish face. Megan has turned her back to him
and pulled the duvet up. Caption box: "I should have made something up."

Panel 3: Cueball alone in frame, lying flat, hands folded on his chest,
staring at the ceiling himself. Caption box: "The Bellman-Ford algorithm
makes terrible pillow talk."
