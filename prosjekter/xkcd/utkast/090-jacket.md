Four panels. The original is one panel with four stacked bubbles; the
vertical format gives each line its own panel. A hallway by a front door,
a row of coat hooks on the wall with two jackets hanging side by side: a
plain dark jacket and, next to it, an identical dark jacket. Cueball is
searching, Blackhat leans against the wall with his arms folded. The
dialogue contains "fucking"; it stays exactly as written. If a provider
refuses, note it in TODONT.md and use the other provider.

Panel 1: Cueball in the middle of the hallway, patting his pockets and
looking around, annoyed. Blackhat against the wall, unbothered. Cueball:
"Where's my fucking jacket?"

Panel 2: Blackhat points lazily at the coat hooks with the two jackets.
Cueball turns to look. Blackhat: "Over there, next to your regular one."

Panel 3: Cueball has stopped, half turned back toward Blackhat, confused.
Blackhat's half-smile. Cueball: "My what?"

Panel 4: Cueball closes his eyes, one hand raised flat, done. Blackhat
looks at the ceiling, innocent. Cueball: "Never mind."
