# Visual canon

Sent to the LLM on the Responses path together with PROMPT.md. Short and
visual: what the model must draw, not how the series feels.

## Format
- Vertical mobile webtoon, 2:3.
- Horizontal panels stacked top to bottom, thin black borders, narrow white
  gutters. The draft states the panel count.
- Polished, warm, modern 3D cartoon style, the same rendering as the
  character cards. Simple uncluttered backgrounds, a limited palette per
  strip. Expressive faces and body language. Adults stay adult. Not stick
  figures, not photoreal.
- English dialogue, spelled exactly as given in the draft.

Character-card images are authoritative for appearance. Ignore the labels
and text printed on the cards. Do not substitute generic people.

## Generation workflow
- Keep scene prompts simple; one setting per strip, identical in every
  panel.
- Provide a character card for EVERY established character in the strip.
  Cards are identity references, not composition.
- Preserve identity across every panel.
