# Image canon for Grok (direct image-model prompts, one panel at a time)

Grok's image_edit drifts toward photoreal rendering when it gets character
cards. The film work showed it follows explicit, repeated style statements;
this file says the look three times in three ways, and names what it must
not become.

One panel of a vertical mobile webtoon, drawn as a single landscape image.
No title, no header, no panel border.

RENDERING, the most important instruction: a hand-drawn digital comic
illustration, exactly like the attached character cards. Clean dark ink
outlines around every figure, face, hand and object. Flat colour fields
with soft painted shading inside the lines. Large expressive eyes,
simplified shapes. It looks like a page from a graphic novel, drawn and
coloured by hand. The character cards ARE the style: copy their line work
and colouring, not only their faces.

NOT a 3D render. No CGI or animated-film look, no plastic or glossy skin,
no ray-traced light, no depth of field, no blurred background. NOT
photorealistic. If the picture could pass for a frame from a 3D animated
film, a video game or a photograph, it is wrong.

The attached character cards are identity and style references, not poses
or composition. Ignore the labels and text on the cards. Every character
looks identical to their card: same face, same body, same clothes.

### Cueball
Slim man, late twenties, completely bald, clean-shaven, dark eyebrows, round
friendly face, grey-blue T-shirt, dark jeans, dark sneakers. Open, puzzled,
sincere.

### Megan
Slim woman, late twenties, dark brown chin-length bob with a side parting,
burgundy long-sleeved top, dark jeans, black sneakers. Calm and deadpan.

### Blackhat
Man, late twenties, black flat-topped pork-pie hat always on, short dark
hair under it, black crew-neck sweater, dark trousers, black boots.
Half-smile, never laughing.

### Erik
Man in his thirties, short light hair, rectangular glasses, light stubble,
ochre sweater, dark jeans, dark sneakers.

### Nora
Woman, late twenties, long dark brown ponytail, petrol-blue long-sleeved
top, light trousers, white sneakers.

Text: English dialogue only, spelled exactly as given. Short speech bubbles
with tails pointing clearly at the speaker, never covering faces. Bubbles
are read top to bottom, then left to right: the line spoken first sits
clearly higher than the reply, and a reply never sits level with or above
the line it answers. Caption boxes are rectangular, at the top of the
panel, no tail. No other text unless the draft asks for it. No sound
effects unless asked.
